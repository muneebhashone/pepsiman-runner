# Pepsiman Runner

## Register

product

## Users

Casual players on desktop and mobile looking for an immediately playable arcade runner.

## Product Purpose

Run through a vivid city, dodge traffic, collect Pepsi cans, complete missions, and beat a personal best. The existing gameplay brief calls for responsive controls, readable hazards, rewarding pickups, and a forgiving difficulty curve.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, playful, confident. Pepsi blue, red, and the silver superhero are the identity anchors.

## Anti-references

The draft's generic glowing panels, featureless block character, empty scenery, and uninformative obstacles. Avoid covering the playable world with decorative UI.

## Design Principles

- Show the actual game from the first screen.
- Prioritize obstacle readability and responsive controls.
- Give every action deliberate animation, sound, and feedback.
- Make failure understandable and retry immediate.
- Keep the game self-contained; no asset-pack dependency.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard, swipe, and touch-button controls; visible focus; readable HUD; sound controls; reduced camera motion; automatic pause when the page loses visibility.
